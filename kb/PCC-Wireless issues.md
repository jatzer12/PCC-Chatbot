Knowledge Base: Connecting to PCC-Wireless WiFi
Keywords: WiFi, PCC-Wireless, internet, login, credentials, connection, wireless

Common Cause
Connection issues for the PCC-Wireless network are frequently caused by incorrect credential formatting or synchronized password errors.

What to Check First
Before reaching out for technical support, ensure your device’s WiFi is turned on and that you are within range of a PCC access point.

Step-by-Step Troubleshooting
Step 1: Verify Your Credentials
To log in, you must use your specific Polynesian Cultural Center (PCC) account details.

Username: Use only the first part of your @polynesia.com email address.

Example: If your email is myersd@polynesia.com, your username is myersd.

Password: Use the unique password you previously set up for your PCC account.

Step 2: Connect to the Network
Open the WiFi settings on your device.

Select PCC-Wireless from the list of available networks.

Enter the username and password verified in Step 1.

If prompted to "Trust" or "Accept" a certificate, select Trust/Yes to complete the connection.

If the Issue Continues
If your credentials are rejected or you cannot establish a connection after verifying your username:

Request a Password Reset: You may need a manual sync or reset from the technical team.

Contact MIS: Send an email to mis@polynesia.com.

Note: When emailing MIS, please include your Full Name and your Username so the team can quickly locate your account and perform the reset.
