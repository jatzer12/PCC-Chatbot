Assets Tag Printer Issues (Not Printing)
keywords: assets tag, printer, printing, print queue, print spooler, tags
Common Cause
Printing issues for the assets tag printer are often caused by a full print queue that stalls the system.
What to Check First
Before restarting the hardware, check if the print queue is full and no tags are being produced.
Step-by-Step Troubleshooting
Step 1: Access System Services
1.	Click the Start menu or search bar on your computer.
2.	Search for "Services".
3.	Right-click on the Services app and select "Run as administrator".
Step 2: Restart the Print Spooler
1.	Once the Services window is open, scroll down to find "Print Spooler".
2.	Right-click on Print Spooler.
3.	Select Restart from the menu.
4.	Wait for the service to refresh and try printing your tags again.
If the Issue Continues
If restarting the Print Spooler does not clear the queue or solve the issue:
1.	Restart your computer.
2.	If the printer still fails to produce tags, please contact the MIS team for further assistance.
